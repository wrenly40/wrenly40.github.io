One new game every Friday

A small arcade, one game at a time.

Every Friday a new game opens here. Each one is a different kind of game from the last, each takes about half an hour to an hour to finish, and each is free, in your browser, with no downloads and no accounts. A game is playable for its week. When the next one arrives, it moves to the Vault: its page, its scores and its medals stay, but it cannot be played again — unless players ask for it back, loudly enough, and often enough.

Paranormal deduction, in ink and paper.

The Night Log

You are the night investigator. Each case file gives you the scene, the timeline, and four witness testimonies. Three of the witnesses are people. The fourth is the thing wearing a person's shape, and its testimony contains one detail that could not be true. Read carefully, and name it.

A full shift is a dozen cases and a finale. Most players finish in half an hour to an hour.
