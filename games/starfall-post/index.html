Starfall Post

You fly the night post: a small courier skiff, one parcel, and an endless lane of falling rock. Fly through the lit rings to deliver — each delivery in a row pays more than the last. Five hits break the parcel and end the run.

Loading the skiff…

How it plays

  • Steer with the arrow keys or WASD, or drag. The ship goes where you point it and stops when you stop.
  • The lane is the same for every player. It starts gentle, then keeps getting faster and busier. Skim a rock closely for a near-miss bonus.
  • Mini-bosses: a wall with one gap, a boulder with circling rocks, a slalom of gates. Survive them for a bonus — they come back harder.
  • Medals come from your best run: bronze at 1,200 m with 4 deliveries, up to the Wren medal in the deep lane.
