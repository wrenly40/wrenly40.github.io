About

One good game a week, or none.

The Wrenworks Arcade is a small games shelf run by Wrenworks, a studio in London. A new game opens every Friday. Every game is a different kind of game — a deduction, a word game, an arcade run, a puzzle — because the variety is the point. Games are free, they run in your browser, and they live only on this site.

A game stays playable for its week, then it rests in the Vault: its page, its final leaderboard and its medals stay for good, but the game itself locks. If enough players ask for a Vault game back — in comments, in emails, in the polls we run — we will hear it, and the numbers will be reported honestly either way.

The quality promise

Every game here has passed the same gate before it opened, and the gate is not a slogan:

  • Someone who has never seen the game plays it cold, start to finish, the way a visitor would. If they cannot finish it unaided, it does not open.
  • Its length is measured, not guessed, and the measured figure is what the game's page says.
  • It has its own finished art and its own sound. No placeholder shapes, no borrowed assets, no silent games.
  • It has a real ending, and a reason to play it again.
  • It works on a phone, or its page says plainly, before the play button, that it wants a keyboard.

And the other half of the promise: if a game is not ready by its Friday, that week has no new game. The calendar bends; the gate does not. A week with nothing new is a better advertisement than a weak game.

The Friday email

If you join the mailing list, you get one email each Friday when the new game opens, and nothing else. Your address is never sold or shared. Unsubscribing is one click, in every email. The first hundred people on the list are the Founding Players; their nicknames, if they want them shown, will be kept on this page.

Privacy, in plain words

  • No accounts. Your passport, your saved games and your scores are kept in your own browser, on your own device. We do not hold passwords because we never ask for one.
  • Leaderboard names are nicknames. Please use one — never your real name, and never an email address. If a name of yours appears anywhere and you want it gone, write to the studio through the main Wrenworks page and it will be removed.
  • Email addresses are held by Kit, our mailing-list provider, for the Friday email only, under the promise above. Joining is double opt-in: Kit emails you a confirmation link, and nothing is sent to the list until you click it.
  • No advertising trackers. We count visits and game finishes in aggregate so we can tell whether the games are any good. That counting carries no advertising cookies, which is why this site has no cookie banner.
