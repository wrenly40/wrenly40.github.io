CaptionTool

Captions for your videos, made on your own computer. Your files never leave your machine.

Drop in a video or audio file. Get back caption-ready .srt and .vtt files, made entirely on your own computer. There is no upload, no account and no subscription. The program has no network code at all, so there is nothing it could send anywhere even if it wanted to.

Who it is for

Anyone who needs captions and does not want to hand their footage to a subscription service. That includes creators with unreleased videos, journalists and lawyers with sensitive recordings, and anyone captioning client work or family footage that is nobody else's business. If your videos are fine to upload, the big subscription services will suit you. CaptionTool is for the rest.

What you get

  • One ready-to-use file for your system: Windows, Mac (Apple silicon) or Linux (Debian/Ubuntu). Double-click to open or install, the way normal software works.
  • Two modes: Accurate, for the best captions it can make, and Fast draft, when you want something quick to tidy up.
  • Captions saved next to your file as .srt and .vtt, the formats YouTube, Vimeo and most editors accept.
  • Caption layout built to readable rules: short lines, no walls of text, and a pace viewers can actually follow.

How it works

Open CaptionTool, choose a file, pick Accurate or Fast draft, and wait while it listens. Both speech engines are built in, so there is nothing else to download or install. On a test with clean solo narration, about 95 words in 100 came out exactly right in Accurate mode. Noisier recordings with music, strong accents or people talking over each other will need more tidying by hand.

Worth knowing before you buy

  • English only in this version.
  • It makes captions only. There are no speaker labels and no built-in subtitle editor, on purpose.
  • The app is not signed with a paid developer certificate, so your system checks with you the first time you open it. On a Mac, open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and click "Open Anyway". On Windows, click "More info" and then "Run anyway". After that it opens normally.
