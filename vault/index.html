The Vault

Games rest here.

Each game in the arcade is playable for its week. When the next game arrives, the old one comes here: its page, its board and its write-up stay, and its medals stay on your record — it just cannot be played again. If players ask for a game back, loudly enough and often enough, we will hear it.
