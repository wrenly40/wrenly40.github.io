HTML5 Horror Game Starter Kit

The full source code of a finished horror game, ready to make your own.

This is not a stub or a half-built demo. The kit is the source of The Last Shift, a top-down horror game we finished and published: you work the night shift in the dark, your torch runs on batteries, and something in the building freezes when your light is on it. The game is complete and playable as it stands. What you are buying is the same code, cleaned up and explained, so you can change the art, the rooms, the tasks and the story and start from a game that already works.

Who it is for

Indie developers and game jam participants who want a working horror game without spending days on the engine underneath. If you can edit HTML and a little JavaScript, you can use this kit. The free demo on the itch.io page is the kit's own game running in the browser, so what you play is what you get.

What you get

  • The whole game in a single HTML file: movement, the battery torch, the stalker, the task checklist, sound, and three nights that get harder.
  • One clearly marked place to change the settings that matter, like battery life, stalker speed and night length, without digging through the code.
  • Comments through the code explaining what each part does and why.
  • A guide that walks you through a quick start, then how to reskin the game, change the tasks and add nights of your own.
  • A licence that lets you use the code in your own games, including games you sell. You just cannot resell the kit itself as a template.
